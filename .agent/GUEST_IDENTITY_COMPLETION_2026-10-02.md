## Implemented

- Account settings, login/logout, identity linking/recovery prompts and Apple authentication dependency/plugin/entitlement retired from app surfaces. New installations create a persistent authenticated private guest; installed historical subjects stay private for ownership compatibility. The public API key is not itself a guest identity. [Supabase anonymous-auth contract](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/auth/auth-anonymous).
- Temporary network failure preserves UUID and leaves offline translation usable. Verified terminal credential loss can create a new guest after isolating old consent, recordings and queues; reinstall/new phone/lost credentials can lose remote balance/data access, as accepted by owner.
- Terms/Privacy and language acceptance are separate from the optional, default-off model-improvement opt-in. Disclosure covers source/results, corrections, ratings, review responses, settings/technical metadata, human review and future model development/commercial use. Specific opt-in requires 18+, a valid owner identity and matching feature flags. Raw audio/transcripts additionally require the default-off speech toggle. Camera photos stay temporary/local.
- Owner-bound consent and durable deletion writes share a serialization lock. Deferred consent completion cannot resurrect sharing after withdrawal or identity replacement. Pending deletion and thirty-day inactivity deny capture/delivery, including direct adapter paths; stale server consent cannot restore local permission.
- Offline known-owner Delete shared data and retry remain reachable. Withdrawal/Delete first stop local sharing, discard owned pending contribution files/rows and persist deletion intent before networking. Server acknowledgement supplies the real original deadline; elapsed deadline alone never means complete. Shared contributions are purged after storage proof while credits, private identity and local history remain. Fresh opt-in waits for actual purge completion. Historical full-identity deletion is preserved as backend history.
- Rewards, purchase and restore accept private guest UUIDs. RevenueCat binding must succeed for the current Supabase subject before StoreKit; subject changes and failed rebinding block purchase. Existing server-verified rewarded receipts remain required; no registered-user bypass or permanent provisional award added.
