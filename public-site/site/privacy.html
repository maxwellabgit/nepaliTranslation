Private identity and providers / निजी पहिचान र प्रदायक

Bola uses a private authenticated guest identity for credits, purchases, authorized sharing and deletion. Contributed content is linked to that identity and is not automatically anonymous or eligible for training or public display. Supabase provides private hosting/storage; Apple and RevenueCat process optional subscriptions; Google AdMob processes ad-related information when network ads are enabled. No advertising identifier (IDFA) is requested. Raw translations, recordings and photos are not sent to third-party analytics.
